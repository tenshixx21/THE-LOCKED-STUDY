The Case

Daniel, a successful business owner, is found dead inside his study at approximately 10:30 PM. Elena, his wife, and Lucas, their son, are the only other people known to have been inside the house.

The study door is locked. There are no obvious signs that someone broke in.

INVESTIGATOR'S NOTE:

Nothing in this case should be accepted at face value. Open every file, compare the times, and decide what the evidence actually proves.
